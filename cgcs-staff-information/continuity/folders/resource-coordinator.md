### Mission Critical Tasks
- Aritcles
- Newsletter
- Website Management
- Mobilization

### Daily Tasks
- Check and respond to emails.
- Check forms on the website: Especially mission trip forms. This will help you know if a trip is filling up or still has spots available. You can then create Instagram and Facebook posts promoting how many spots are left.
- Post on Instagram Stories: Post links to our podcast, articles, mission trips, or promote events coming up.
- Engage with students and other SEBTS pages on Instagram: Like posts, comment on posts, and respond to any messages.

### Weekly Tasks

#### Newsletter
- Write down resources used from the week before
- Duplicate on Active Campaign and prep for new week
- Collect resources from Southern Baptist missions’ outlets
  - Every now and then uses websites like Radical and Missionary
- Choose an article from Keelan’s website for the intro to newsletter.
  - Make sure it fits wording wise
- Prayer card
- Send to TA to proofread
- Schedule for Friday at 8am and click FINISH twice


#### Articles
- Create articles:
  - Edit
  - Format in Worpress
  - Find header photo from IMB or Unsplash
    - Communicate with writers about: 
    - Edits
    - Titles
    - Headshots, bios
    - When their article will be posted 
    - Encouragement if we want them to write for us again
  - Create author user profile
    - Headshot
    - Short bio
    - Email
- Reaching out to new writers.
  - Typically, people connected to SEBTS
  - Past Jman
  - Students on the field
  - Field workers who graduated from SEBTS
  - Doctoral students love it
  - Students who just returned from M trip
  - Send them email on tips for writing for CGCS 
  - Trying to have articles lined up at least one month in advance
- Asking old writers to consider writing
- Creating article line up
-	Getting articles to Madison 
  - Title
  - Name
  - Date
  - Headshot
  - Header image
  - If they want to collab on Insta, then their handles


#### Mobilization team
- Biweekly Mobilizer meeting
- Reaching out to students about Go Meetings
- Scheduling Go Meetings
- Meeting with students
- Making notes on students in HubSpot

#### Newsletter Sign-up Stickers
- When I get a notification for a long distance signee, I address and stuff envelope. 
- Get stamp from Kelly or charge stamp to cgcs account at Locker
- Mail at the Locker


### Monthly Tasks
- Schedule article content calendar
- Brainstorm content for articles
- Reach out to blog contributors: Email previous contributors, professors, students, or alumni who could write an article for our blog. You may need to come up with topic ideas for some contributors.


### Semester Tasks
Prepare for upcoming events each semester: In the Fall, our big event is Gathering Chapel. In the Spring, our big events include Global Missions Week and Commissioning Chapel.

#### Global Missions Week
- You or Comms create graphics for each GMW event. I have already created GMW 2025 graphics in Adobe.
- Edit and GMW page on the website with correct dates, times, locations, descriptions, and sign-ups.
- Help write and send target emails to students for GMW events (from CGCS).
- Help plan and execute events.

#### Commissioning Chapel
- Help prepare sending list for Commissioning Chapel
- Help create emails to invite missionaries to the chapel

#### Other Semester Tasks
-  Help write & edit marketing emails to students

## Lessons Learned
1. Ask for help or clarification! Don’t feel like you have to figure everything out by yourself.
2. Play around with the website: Events, Forms, Pages, Posts, Users
3. For articles:
    - Go look at the authors from previous articles on our website. That will give you a good idea of who to reach out to for writing for our blog. Also, if a team goes on a mission trip, ask a student or two to write about their experience! When telling them what to write, you can refer to our [Blog Style Guide](https://www.thecgcs.org/blog-style-guide/) on the website!

## What to do during your first week of work!
- Get your position trainer to help you get set up on the website. This may take a couple of weeks of learning it and playing around with it, but that’s okay. Ask for help.
- Get Kelly or Timothy to help you set up your email signature.
