## Tasks
Create a list of daily, weekly, monthly, semesterly, and mission critical tasks. These can include meetings to attend, reports and due dates, milestones, or subscription renewal dates. Include details about how to accomplish these tasks.

### Mission Critical Tasks
- Social Media/Promotions
- Articles
- Podcast Uploads
- Website Management

### Daily Tasks
- Check and respond to emails.
- Create content for Facebook posts: This includes Verses, Quotes, Promotions for Mission Trips, Ministry Opportunities, World Missions Clock, Prayer Requests, Podcast, Articles, etc. Maybe include up to 6 posts a day. You can schedule all the posts through Hootsuite.
- Check forms on the website: Especially mission trip forms. This will help you know if a trip is filling up or still has spots available. You can then create Instagram and Facebook posts promoting how many spots are left.
- Post on Instagram Stories: Post links to our podcast, articles, mission trips, or promote events coming up.
- Engage with students and other SEBTS pages on Instagram: Like posts, comment on posts, and respond to any messages.

### Weekly Tasks

#### Podcast
- Create graphics for our weekly podcast episode: Use Adobe Express to create two graphics (one for Soundcloud and one for our website)
- Create a post on our website for our weekly podcast episode: Schedule a post on our website for a new episode to drop **every Wednesday at 3:00am**, then create a Facebook post to promote the new episode at 9:00am.
- After the podcast has been uploaded on Wednesday, go to The Sent Life Podcast page on the website and replace the “Listen to the latest episode” box with the new episode. Repeat every week.
- Post new podcast on Instagram stories sometime during the week and repost the podcast on Facebook another day (maybe Friday) of the week.

#### Articles
- Create a post on our website for our weekly articles: We will publish an article **every Friday at 3:00am**, then create a Facebook post to promote the new article at 9:00am. Make sure the article is proofread and edited before publishing.
- Email the author of the article two days in advance to remind them you are publishing their article that week.
- Post the new article on Instagram Stories at some point during the week and then repost on Facebook at the beginning of the next week.

#### Social Media
- Post on Instagram 2-3 times a week
- Post on Instagram Stories daily
- Post on Facebook 4-6 times daily.
  - You can use Hootsuite to schedule these posts.


### Monthly Tasks
- Schedule social media Calendar: At the beginning of each month make a calendar of what you will post.
- Brainstorm content for social media: Think of creative ways for engagement (reels, cool posts, stories) Be creative!
- Reach out to blog contributors: Email previous contributors, professors, students, or alumni who could write an article for our blog. You may need to come up with topic ideas for some contributors.

### Semester Tasks
Prepare for upcoming events each semester: In the Fall, our big event is Gathering Chapel. In the Spring, our big events include Global Missions Week and Commissioning Chapel.

#### Gathering Chapel:
-  Request Comms to make slides for all mission trips
-  Request Comms to make a general Gathering Chapel promo graphic/slide
-  Get all mission trip information for the website (dates, leaders, location, description for website)
-  Put all graphics and mission trip info on the website the day before Gathering Chapel
-  Make sure the mission trip interest form works and is under every mission trip on the website
-  Create or get comms to create a graphic with a QR code to the interest form (use Adobe Express if you want to make yourself)
-  Make sure media and the head of chapel has all the graphics before Gathering Chapel
-  Promote the Gathering Chapel on Social Media
-  Help create a new mission trip t-shirt before Gathering Chapel. Do this with the design team in Comms. Make sure Kelly orders the shirts.
-  Take pictures of service and request Comms to get photography of chapel service.
-  Post a recap of the chapel and about all the mission trips/interest form

#### Global Missions Week
-  Get Comms to create and send you main GMW graphics
-  You or Comms create graphics for each GMW event. I have already created GMW 2025 graphics in Adobe.
-  Edit and GMW page on the website with correct dates, times, locations, descriptions, and sign-ups.
-  Promote GMW events in advance on social media
-  Help write and send target emails to students for GMW events (from CGCS)
-  Ask Comms to send out a student-wide email to students about GMW (from Dr. Akin)
-  Help plan and execute events
-  Take pictures and videos from GMW events
-  Post about events throughout the week

#### Commissioning Chapel
-  Help prepare sending list for Commissioning Chapel
-  Get Comms to create graphics for Commissioning Chapel
-  You can also create graphics in Adobe or ask Comms for slides
-  Promote Commissioning Chapel on social media
-  Help create emails to invite missionaries to the chapel
-  Make sure Media is prepared with all graphics before Commissioning Chapel
-  Take pictures and videos (be careful of getting people’s faces-they may be going to secure places)
-  Ask Comms to take pictures of the chapel and warn them about the security. Ask them to take pictures of the back of people’s heads or more secure pics
-  Post about the chapel after

#### GO Conference
-  Promote the Conference months in advance
-  Ask Comms to send you all of their graphics and promotion timeline
-  Work with Comms/Collaborate on social media posts
-  Take videos and pictures during Conference
-  Post on social media throughout the Conference

#### Other Semester Tasks
-  Promote Mission Trips throughout the semester
-  Help write & edit marketing emails to students

## Lessons Learned
1. Ask for help or clarification! Don’t feel like you have to figure everything out by yourself.
2. Play around with the website: Events, Forms, Pages, Posts, Users
3. For articles:
  - When you create a new user that an author, unfortunately you have to include the author’s email in the profile. Once you do this, it will send that person an email from our website to set up their password. They do not need access to our website. I always email them right before submitting their author profile to let them know to ignore the email from our website.
  - Go look at the authors from previous articles on our website. That will give you a good idea of who to reach out to for writing for our blog. Also, if a team goes on a mission trip, ask a student or two to write about their experience! When telling them what to write, you can refer to our [Blog Style Guide](https://www.thecgcs.org/blog-style-guide/){:target="_blank"} on the website!
